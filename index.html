Our Story

Built for people who dress with intent.

I started ARIVX in 2026 because good streetwear in India was either too expensive or too plain. I wanted oversized fits, dark colours and real graphics without a luxury-brand price tag.

Every design is chosen with one question: would I wear this every week? If the answer is no, it does not make the drop. That is why we launched with just five tees.

ARIVX is young and independent, and I answer your WhatsApp messages myself. Tell me what you need and I will help you pick.

  • Original graphics, not recycled print-shop templates

  • Honest pricing, from ₹399 to ₹599
